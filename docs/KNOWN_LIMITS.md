# Scope and limitations

The main theorem is a congruence modulo p² for every prime p>3. It is not a
modulo-p³ result. The original sum has denominator 54; the companion Franel sum
has denominator 50 and is restricted to p>5.

The invariant lifting lemma requires integral coefficients and a p-integral
parameter t0 with t0D(t0) a unit. Its nonsingularity condition cannot be dropped:
the companion includes a singular counterexample. The split specialisation
requires the displayed exact root to be a p-adic unit; it is proved to be one
under the selected embedding, rather than assumed from the desired congruence.

The coefficient certificates prove eta identities only in combination with
the manuscript's modularity, cusp-holomorphy and valence argument. Prime scans,
sample modular polynomials and negative controls are finite regressions.
The optional symbolic checks do not formalise the modular or p-adic reasoning.

The paper gives a proof of the statement labelled Conjecture 3.5 by Sun. It does
not claim that this is the first proof in the literature. The bibliographic
search is targeted; several recent related full texts have not been fully
compared. See [source comparison](SOURCE_COMPARISON.md).

AI-assisted checking is disclosed in [AI_USE](../AI_USE.md). The manuscript
has not undergone specialist human refereeing or proof-assistant verification.
These review limits are distinct from a missing mathematical step.
